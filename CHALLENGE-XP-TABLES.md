# Forge challenge XP — every figure, as implemented

Generated from the shipped engine by `tools/xp-audit/challenge-xp-tables.mjs`.
Each challenge is previewed exactly as the Challenge Library previews it, so
these are the figures the app advertises **and** awards.

Difficulty sets a daily XP budget and the requirements divide it, so a
challenge is worth its tier per day regardless of how many boxes it has.

| Rate | Value |
|---|---|
| Daily budget by tier | Easy 120 · Medium 160 · Medium/Hard 180 · Hard 200 · Elite 220 |
| Mode multiplier | Beginner 0.85 · Standard 1 · Hard 1.08 |
| Perfect day | 15% of the daily budget |
| Completion bonus | 20% × budget × days |
| Weekly requirements | 50% of the daily budget, per week |
| Optional (Bonus Mission) XP | 10% of budget per day, 5% × budget × days per attempt |
| Streak milestones | 3d +20 · 7d +50 · 14d +80 · 30d +150 · 60d +250 · 75d +300, capped at 5% × budget × days |
| Missed day | −50% of the daily budget |
| Missed single requirement | −25% of that requirement |
| Keystone weights | 0★ 1 · 1★ 1.3 · 2★ 2 · 3★ 3 |
